# Integral School

**Learning with people, nature and AI.**

Integral School is a model for a school day with three teachers: other people, the natural world, and AI learning interfaces. It is written for schools, families, communities and anyone who builds learning tools. Any part of it can be adopted on its own.

## Why

Traditional classrooms and lesson design are outdated. Knowledge is everywhere, and learning can happen indoors, outdoors, at home and on the move.

## A day in the integrated classroom

- **Start outdoors, together, off screen.** The group begins the day with something it builds or does as one.
- **Learning pods.** Comfortable places where learners sit down, relax, follow their lesson plans and are carried through the day's learning journey.
- **A guide each learner chooses.** Virtual teaching avatars can be personalised. They help learners choose what to learn and when, with different pathways, menus and flash cards for all levels and abilities, and backup plans and cognitive workarounds for those who need them.
- **Human teachers, always around.** They guide, make sure the learning loop works for everyone, and step in to solve problems and answer the questions the interface cannot handle.
- **Short sessions.** Sessions start at 30 to 45 minutes, with relaxation breaks every 20 minutes or so, and get shorter through the day.
- **Back into the world.** What is learned in the pod is found again outside: number in petals and shells, geometry in honeycomb, physics in water, wind and the cosmos, history in the places people built.
- **Augmented reality and wearables, if switched on.** They can explain what a learner is looking at: the science, the history, the economics, the social context, even the poetry.
- **Afternoons away from the interface.** Sports, music, gardening, practical labs, and time to talk, play and watch clouds.

## Who it is for

The model is written with the early years in mind and applies to every age and setting: schools, universities, home education and lifelong learning, in a building or remote.

## Status

Integral School is a proposal to be piloted and evaluated. It makes no claim of proven results, and it invites schools and communities to test parts of it and report what they find.
